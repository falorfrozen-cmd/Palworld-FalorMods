[h1]Passive Transfer[/h1]
Move one passive skill from a Pal in your party to another Pal in your party. The giver loses the passive, so nothing is duplicated.

[h2]How it works[/h2]
[list]
[*]Press [b]F5[/b] in a world to open the panel (the key can be changed).
[*]Pick the giver, the passive, then the receiver, and press [b]Transfer[/b].
[*]If the receiver already has 4 passives, you pick one of them and it goes back to the giver (a swap).
[*]Every step is read back from the game. If a step fails, the earlier steps are undone.
[*]Panel language: English or Turkish. It follows the game language, or you can pick one.
[/list]

[h2]Required items[/h2]
[list]
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3625223587]UE4SS Experimental (Palworld)[/url]
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3771664251]Mod Options Framework[/url] (settings page: Esc > Mod Options)
[/list]
PalSchema is not needed.

Install them from the Steam Workshop only. Do not keep a manually installed UE4SS next to the Workshop one (for example a dwmapi.dll or a ue4ss folder under Pal\Binaries\Win64). Two UE4SS copies load at once and the game crashes at startup.

[h2]Install[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Subscribe to this mod and to the two required items.
[*]Start Palworld, open Options > Mod Management, enable all three and save. The game restarts.
[*]Load a world you host and press F5.
[/olist]

[h2]Settings[/h2]
Esc > Mod Options > Passive Transfer
[list]
[*]Enabled
[*]Open / close key (default F5; a key change applies after restarting the game)
[*]Panel language (Auto / English / Türkçe)
[/list]

[h2]Limits - please read[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]World host only.[/b] It works in single player and for the host of a co-op world. A guest in someone else's world sees "Only the world host can use this." Dedicated servers are not supported.
[*]Both Pals must be in your party (the 5 party slots).
[*]Every passive can be moved, including rare ones. There is no cost and no cooldown.
[*]Changes are written into the save. Removing the mod does not undo transfers you already made. Back up your save first if you are unsure.
[/list]

[h2]Tested[/h2]
Palworld 1.0 on Steam (Windows), as world host: transfer, swap with a full receiver, and the saved result after a restart.
Not tested: dedicated servers.
